# Model Router Instructions for Copilot

Use `docs/provider-neutral-policy.md` as the governing routing policy.
Classify engineering work as SIMPLE, NORMAL, or COMPLEX and map it to
primary, standard, or advanced. Follow `docs/handoff-protocol.md` when a task
is delegated or transferred.

Copilot capabilities vary by editor, account, and mode. Do not claim that a
worker was started, reused, transferred, or closed unless the host exposes and
confirms that lifecycle. When it does not, remain the sole owner in the current
session and report the fallback.

Use `docs/validation-matrix.md` for acceptance checks and include the selected
lane and known model/profile in the completion report.
